A certified, registered, tradable asset class

Every Aluxria cabin is a certified building under the National Construction Code, registered with the local government authority, and stewarded for 50 years under the Aluxria Care framework.

The Care framework does not invent a new standard — it wraps a structured ownership, condition and resale architecture around an existing statutory certification. Two independent layers verify the asset without reference to the manufacturer; a third documents its life.
01
Independent statutory layer

NCC certification & Occupation Certificate

The building is signed off by an accredited certifier as compliant with the National Construction Code at the applicable class. An Occupation Certificate is issued by the relevant authority before the asset is brought into use. The same regulatory instrument that certifies every house, hotel and apartment building in Australia — verifiable on the public record, independent of Aluxria.

02
Independent public register

Council / LGA fixed-asset register

Each cabin is recorded on the local government authority's fixed-asset register by chassis number, tied to the Occupation Certificate. A third-party public record — the instrument that gives lenders and insurers confidence in titled and registered asset classes. Verifiable without reliance on the manufacturer.

03
Manufacturer-issued lifecycle layer

Aluxria Asset Passport & Care Score

Tamper-evident digital identity carried by every cabin from build through resale — manufacture, install, service, upgrade, movement and ownership records. Condition is expressed as a transparent, point-based Care Score, maintained by the Certified Partner Network under independent Standards Board governance. Designed to be underwritable.

The substance

The statutory layer is fixed. Aluxria Care is the wrap that turns a certified building into a financeable, tradable asset across its full 50-year life.

Component 00  ·  The foundation the framework wraps

National Construction Code certification

Every Aluxria cabin is certified under the National Construction Code by an accredited building certifier and brought into use under an Occupation Certificate issued by the consent authority. It is then recorded on the local government authority's fixed-asset register by chassis number. The Aluxria Care framework wraps this existing statutory layer rather than substituting for it — the building is independently certified before any Aluxria-issued document touches it.

Certifier sign-off

Accredited under state-based building practitioner regimes; same regulatory pathway as conventional construction.

Occupation Certificate

Issued by the consent authority before occupation — public record of code compliance.

LGA fixed-asset register

Recorded by chassis number; third-party verifiable; independent of the manufacturer.

Component 01

Asset Passport

Digital identity carried by every Aluxria asset, physically embedded via QR / NFC at manufacture. Tamper-evident, API-ready, designed for institutional read-access from inception.

  • Manufacture, engineering, NCC compliance and install records (incl. OC reference)
  • Full service, inspection, upgrade, movement and ownership history
  • Australian data residency; multi-region architecture for export
Component 02

Asset Care Score

A transparent, published, point-based measure of stewardship. Cumulative across ownership transfers and weighted by Care tier — condition expressed as a portable, underwritable number.

Component 03

Certified Partner Network

The operational backbone. Regional installation, inspection and service capability delivered through a four-stage certification pathway with documented competency milestones.

  • Certified Installers, Inspectors, Maintenance Partners and Service Coordinators
  • Trainee → Certified → Senior Certified → Regional Service Coordinator
  • Three service pathways calibrated into the Care Score
Component 04

Resale, Finance & Insurance Ecosystem

The mechanism that converts Passport data and Care Score into measurable financial value. Built API-first to feed institutional partners directly via authenticated, audited access.

  • Certified Pre-Owned: re-certified condition, full Passport disclosure, warranty calibrated to Care Score at resale
  • Resale into matching climate and wind-rating regions
  • Lender and insurer APIs: Passport and Care Score referenced in terms and premiums
Component 05

Governance & Standards Board

An independent board governs the standards on which the financeability of the asset class depends. Arm's-length from commercial pressure — the structural feature that allows institutional partners to rely on the framework.

  • Owns the Asset Care Schedule, Care Score methodology and certification curriculum
  • Independent chair; engineering, consumer-advocacy and adjacent-industry representation
  • Annual public reporting; documented dispute resolution and independent review
Component 06

Warranty

Seven years standard, extendable to ten or more under higher Care tiers. Eligibility is tied to documented care and causation analysis rather than blanket conditions — defensible under Australian Consumer Law and aligned with how reasonable warranty terms are interpreted by regulators and courts.

  • Claim assessment runs against Passport service history and current Care Score
  • Dispute pathway via independent Standards Board; outcomes recorded with reasoning
A certified building under the National Construction Code — registered on a public local government register, identified by a manufacturer-issued lifecycle Passport, scored on a transparent condition index, resold through a certified channel, and stewarded under independent governance for fifty years. Three verification layers. One financeable asset class.
