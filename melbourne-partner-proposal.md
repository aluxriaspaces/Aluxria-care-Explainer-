## 1. Purpose

This proposal sets out a working commercial structure for a Melbourne partnership between Aluxria and [Partner]. The intent is a clean division of responsibility: Aluxria supplies precision-built, certified modular cabins; the partner supplies local site, installation, upgrade works and market presence. Both parties sell into the Melbourne market.

The structure below is presented for discussion. Figures quoted are the commercial terms supplied to date; items marked **To confirm** require agreement before this becomes a binding arrangement.

## 3. Cabin supply & pricing

- **Wholesale supply price:** Aluxria supplies each cabin to [Partner] at **$110,000**.
- **Retail price:** [Partner] retails the cabin at **$138,000**.
- **Unit margin to [Partner]:** **$28,000** per cabin (≈20.3% of retail), before upgrades and installation revenue.

Every cabin is supplied as a certified building under the National Construction Code and carries the Aluxria Care framework (Asset Passport, Care Score, 50-year service architecture). This is a quality and lifecycle position, not a discount position — the cabin is sold as precision-built, financeable infrastructure.

> **To confirm:**
> - Whether the $110k / $138k figures are GST-inclusive or ex-GST.
> - Whether the wholesale price is fixed for the term or indexed (e.g. annual review, freight/material movements).
> - Delivery point and freight responsibility (ex-works factory vs delivered to Fitzroy / site).
> - Minimum volume or stocking commitment, if any.

---

## 4. Site, installation & upgrades — [Partner]

[Partner] supplies and is responsible for:

- **Fitzroy site** — display and customer-facing presence.
- **Installation** — site works and placement of the cabin.
- **Upgrades** — including **decks** and **flyover roofs**, and other customer-specified enhancements.

Installation and upgrade works are [Partner]'s to price, deliver and warrant, and represent additional revenue to [Partner] on top of the $28k unit margin.

> **To confirm:**
> - That installation and upgrade works are carried out by certified personnel and sign-off is documented to the Aluxria Asset Passport, so the cabin's Care record and warranty position are preserved.
> - Whether [Partner] joins the Aluxria Certified Partner Network for installation/upgrade certification, or works under an agreed equivalent standard.
> - Where responsibility sits for site-specific approvals, permits and any Occupation Certificate steps at Fitzroy and at customer sites.

## 5. Melbourne show

[Partner] funds the Melbourne show entry and stand at **$35,000**. This covers the partner's event entry and display presence; it is an event/marketing cost borne by [Partner], not a fee payable to Aluxria.

> **To confirm:**
> - Which show, dates and stand specification the $35k covers.
> - Whether the display unit is a sold cabin, a loan/demo unit, or counted against the partner's stock.
> - Aluxria's in-kind contribution (brand assets, collateral, on-stand support), if any.

## 6. Sales — both parties

Both Aluxria and [Partner] sell cabins into the Melbourne market. The mechanics of how a sale is attributed and fulfilled need to be agreed so the arrangement stays clean as volume grows.

> **To confirm — this is the main open commercial point:**
> - **Partner-originated sale:** [Partner] sells at $138k, having bought at $110k — margin and install/upgrade revenue sit with [Partner]. (Straightforward.)
> - **Aluxria-originated sale in Melbourne:** How is this handled? Options to decide between —
>   1. Aluxria refers the lead to [Partner], who fulfils and pays the $110k wholesale (partner keeps margin).
>   2. Aluxria sells direct and pays [Partner] an installation/site fee for fulfilment.
>   3. A referral commission either way.
> - Whether the Fitzroy/Melbourne territory is exclusive to [Partner], and if so on what terms.

## 8. Why this structure works

- **Clear division of responsibility.** Aluxria controls manufacture, certification and the Care framework; [Partner] controls local site, installation, upgrades and market presence. Each party does what it does best.
- **Quality preserved end to end.** Provided installation and upgrade works are documented to the Asset Passport, the cabin's certification, Care Score and warranty position carry through to the customer and into resale.
- **Aligned upside.** Both parties sell, both parties benefit from a strong Melbourne presence, and the partner captures margin plus installation and upgrade revenue.
