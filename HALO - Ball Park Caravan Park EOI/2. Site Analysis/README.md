# 2. Site Analysis  🔴 Priority

**Purpose:** Establish what physically exists and its condition — the basis for every
redevelopment and financial assumption.

## Capture
- [ ] Total area (public est. ~12 acres / ~4.86 ha — **confirm**), developable area,
      river frontage length
- [ ] Counts: powered sites, unpowered sites, cabins, amenities blocks, camp kitchens,
      roads, boat ramps, jetties
- [ ] Condition assessment: amenities, roads, drainage, landscaping, electrical,
      sewer, water
- [ ] **Utilities capacity** (§8): power, sewer, water, internet
- [ ] Expansion headroom: additional cabins / floating accommodation / event spaces

## Inputs
Site plans (folder 1), a physical **site inspection**, aerial imagery, and
utility/servicing advice. Photograph and date everything.

## Maps to checklist
§2 (site analysis) and §8 (infrastructure requirements).
