# 8. Investors

**Purpose:** Map potential capital partners for a $15–20M+ redevelopment over a long
lease term — to test whether the investment can realistically be raised (part of the
master question).

## Research targets
- [ ] **Tourism / accommodation investors** (funds, family offices, REIT-adjacent)
- [ ] **Riverina** regional investors
- [ ] **Albury / Wodonga** developers and contractors
- [ ] Government co-investment: regional tourism / growth funds (state & federal)

## For each
- [ ] Mandate & cheque size
- [ ] Appetite for regional tourism / long-lease assets
- [ ] Key contacts
- [ ] What they would need to see (returns, security, lease term)

## Discipline
No investor is to be represented as committed until terms are documented. Capital
strategy must align with the modelling in folder 6.
