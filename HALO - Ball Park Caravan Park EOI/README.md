# HALO — Ball Park Caravan Park EOI

Preliminary investigation and EOI workspace for the redevelopment and long-term
operation of **Ball Park Caravan Park, Corowa NSW** — a Federation Council
Expression of Interest opportunity on the Murray River.

> **Brand context:** Aluxria — *Luxury Spaces in All Places.* Premium, scalable
> modular accommodation; regenerative development; workforce and tourism
> infrastructure; respectful Indigenous partnership; long-term stewardship.

---

## The master question

> **Can we create a destination that justifies a 50–60 year stewardship lease and
> attracts the investment needed to transform the site into a flagship Murray
> River tourism asset?**
>
> If **yes** — we proceed. If **no** — we walk away before committing significant
> time or money.

Everything in this workspace exists to answer that question with evidence, not
optimism.

## Working principles

- **Verify before you rely.** Public reporting is a starting point, not a source of
  truth for a submission. Confirm every figure against the official EOI pack.
- **No fabrication.** No certifications, approvals, partnerships, or financial
  returns are to be represented as secured until they are.
- **Flag assumptions.** Anything not yet confirmed is marked ⚠️ and lives in the
  assumptions register.
- **Decision discipline.** We are testing a go / no-go decision, not pre-committing
  to a bid.
