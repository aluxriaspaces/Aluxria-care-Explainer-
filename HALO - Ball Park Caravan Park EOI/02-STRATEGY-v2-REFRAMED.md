# Ball Park Caravan Park — Strategy v2 (reframed to EOI 2026-305)

**Aluxria / HALO · with Rock Solid Design & Construction** · 30 May 2026
*One-page strategic position, corrected against the official EOI. Supersedes v1 where
they differ (see `00-STRATEGY-REVIEW.md`).*

---

## The opportunity

Federation Council seeks a long-term operator–developer for **Ball Park Caravan Park,
Corowa** — ~8 ha of Murray River frontage (mixed Crown/Council tenure), with an active
park plus an **unapproved premium riverfront "overflow" area** earmarked as Stage 1 of
Council's 2020 Masterplan. A genuine chance to build a flagship Murray River destination.

## Our position

Aluxria leads a **consortium** that brings precision-built modular accommodation,
construction delivery and lifecycle stewardship, alongside an **experienced holiday-park
operator** and an **investment partner** — a credible answer to Council's heaviest
evaluation criterion (Capability & Experience, **35%**).

## Lease & stewardship — within the term on offer

- We propose to build on the **21-year initial term**, structured to be **viable on 21
  years**, and welcome the **30-year extension** (subject to Ministerial approval) as
  upside.
- Our **stewardship model** delivers the long-term reinvestment Council wants *within*
  this term: staged capital works, a committed **maintenance fund**, and **Aluxria Care**
  lifecycle management (asset passport, condition scoring, scheduled maintenance) so the
  asset is handed back in strong condition.
- *(The 50–60 year aspiration in v1 is not available under the Crown Land Management Act
  in this process and has been removed.)*

## Positioning (calibrated to the adopted Masterplan)

**Not** a luxury resort. We deliver **Council's adopted Masterplan and its economic
target — ~$2.88M → ~$5.6M/yr (≈ +$2.72M/yr), Council's own business-case figure** —
faster and to a higher standard, via precision-built modular accommodation. Premium is
the **yield-enhancing layer**, not the headline. The Masterplan roughly **doubles**
capacity (139 approved sites → ~300 ambition); we present that as flood-informed,
approvals-subject *targets*, not entitlements. *(See `03-MASTERPLAN-ANALYSIS.md`.)*

## Vision (aligned to Council's objectives)

A staged, year-round destination integrating premium modular cabins, modernised caravan/
RV and camping, riverfront activation, family recreation, cultural tourism in genuine
partnership with Traditional Owners, and sustainability (solar, water recycling, waste
reduction — all called out in the EOI objectives).

## Staging

| Stage | Focus | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Modernise the operating park; new amenities; first premium modular cabins | Keep park trading; addresses variable-condition amenity blocks |
| 2 | Riverfront accommodation precinct (overflow area) | **Lessee-led DA/S68/Crown approvals** — primary development risk |
| 3 | Floating accommodation **pilot** + waterfront activation | **Upside, contingent** on maritime/flood/Crown feasibility — not load-bearing |
| 4 | Event & wellness facilities; destination expansion | Demand-led |

## Consortium structure

- **Aluxria** — vision, development strategy, modular accommodation, stewardship (Care).
- **Rock Solid Design & Construction** — construction delivery (citable track record).
- **Operating Partner** — holiday-park operations, staffing, bookings, guest services.
  *Targets: Hampshire, NRMA Parks & Resorts, Discovery, Ingenia, Tasman, BIG4/G'Day.*
  *(Reflections excluded — NSW Crown land manager, likely competitor.)*
- **Investment Partner** — equity/funding (tourism investors, regional developers,
  family offices).
- **Tourism integration** — Destination Riverina Murray / Murray Regional Tourism.

## Required commercial decisions (per EOI)

- **Lease term election:** state 21 yrs / 30 yrs / contingent-on-30.
- **Council cabins C1–C20** (>$500k, return expected): **buy or lease** — to be decided.
- **Maintenance-fund %** and rent/review approach — firmed at Stage 2 (RFDP).

## Go / no-go gates (proceed only if all YES)

1. **Viability** on a 21-yr term, absorbing lessee-borne overflow development cost.
2. **Operator capability** secured (LOI by 26 June) for the 35% criterion.
3. **Investment appetite** for a multi-$M, long-but-finite lease.
