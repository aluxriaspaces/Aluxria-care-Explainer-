## What we need from an operating partner (Stage 1)

1. Willingness to be **named** in the EOI as intended operating partner.
2. A short **capability statement** + 2–3 **reference projects/parks** (their material).
3. A light **letter of intent / in-principle interest** — not a binding agreement.
4. Indicative view on operating model and any commercial expectations (for Stage 2).

## What we offer a partner

- A **shovel-ready opportunity** already scoped, with the EOI work done.
- **Precision-built modular accommodation** + **construction delivery** (Rock Solid) —
  faster, staged, lower trade risk; park keeps trading.
- **Aluxria Care** lifecycle stewardship — an asset-condition story Councils value.
- A structured consortium where the operator does what it does best (operations) without
  carrying development/construction risk alone.
