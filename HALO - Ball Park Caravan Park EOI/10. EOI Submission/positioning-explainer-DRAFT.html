Murray River tourism redevelopment · Long-term lease

Aluxria proposes to operate, redevelop and steward Ball Park Caravan Park as a premium Murray River destination — precision-built, regeneratively planned, and managed for the full term of the lease.

Federation Council is seeking a long-term operating and redevelopment partner for an iconic waterfront holiday park. Aluxria brings a delivery-capable model that combines scalable, factory-built accommodation with a structured stewardship framework — designed to realise the Council's master plan ambition on a credible program, with quality, sustainability and community outcomes built in from the outset.
01
The accommodation

Precision-built modular cabins

Factory-built to the National Construction Code under controlled quality conditions, then installed on site with minimal disruption to existing operations. Modular delivery compresses the construction program, reduces weather and trade risk, and allows the park to be staged so it can keep trading through redevelopment. Premium accommodation — not a cheap alternative.

02
The place

Regenerative waterfront destination

A masterplanned upgrade that respects the river setting and the park's standing in Corowa: improved visitor facilities, landscaping and amenity, energy and water efficiency, and a mix of accommodation that extends the season and the average length of stay. Designed to grow visitation and strengthen the local economy.

03
The stewardship

Managed for the full lease term

Every cabin carries the Aluxria Care framework — a digital asset passport, transparent condition scoring and a structured maintenance schedule — so the asset is documented and maintained across decades, not just delivered. Council retains a partner accountable for condition and presentation for the life of the lease.

The proposal in substance

A delivery-capable operator that meets the Council's objectives on a credible program — with quality, sustainability and community value built into the model.

Alignment  ·  What Federation Council is seeking

Modernise the destination · grow tourism · strengthen the local economy

The Council's stated objectives are to modernise visitor facilities, attract investment, increase visitation and extend stays at a significant Murray River asset. Aluxria's model is built around exactly these outcomes: a staged redevelopment that lifts amenity and accommodation standards while the park keeps trading, delivered by a partner accountable for presentation and condition across the full lease term.

Modernise & invest

Precision-built accommodation and upgraded facilities, delivered on a program aligned to the master plan's scope.

Grow visitation

A broader accommodation mix and improved amenity to extend the season and lengthen the average stay.

Local economic value

Regional supply, local employment and visitor spend that flows into Corowa and the surrounding district.

Component 01

Accommodation offer

A staged mix of premium modular cabins and upgraded sites, scaled to the park's layout and the river frontage.

  • NCC-certified cabins, factory-built under controlled quality conditions
  • Range of cabin types to broaden the market and lift yield
  • Retained and upgraded powered and camping sites
Component 02

Regeneration & sustainability

Environmental outcomes tied to operating benefits — lower running costs and a more resilient destination.

  • Energy- and water-efficient design; solar and storage where viable
  • Landscaping and riverfront amenity that respect the setting
  • Waste, stormwater and flood-aware site planning
Component 03

Indigenous partnership

Genuine, collaborative engagement with Traditional Owners and local Aboriginal organisations, shaped through consultation rather than assumed.

  • Cultural heritage assessment and respectful site interpretation
  • Pathways for training, employment and supply
  • Shared outcomes built on stewardship and cultural integrity
Component 04

Workforce & community

Local employment and capability through delivery and operation, with benefits retained in the district.

  • Local and regional trades engaged through staged works
  • Operational roles in management, housekeeping and grounds
  • Accommodation that can also serve regional workforce demand
Component 05

Delivery, compliance & program

A staged approach that keeps the park trading, with planning and approvals managed through the standard NSW pathway.

  • Development consent and certification via the consent authority
  • Early works first; cabins installed in defined stages
  • Program and milestones reported transparently to Council
Component 06

Commercial model & stewardship

A long-term lease structure that aligns Aluxria's investment with Council's return and the asset's condition over time.

  • Lease and return structure to be agreed with Council
  • Aluxria Care: passport, condition scoring and maintenance schedule
  • Asset documented and maintained to handback standard
