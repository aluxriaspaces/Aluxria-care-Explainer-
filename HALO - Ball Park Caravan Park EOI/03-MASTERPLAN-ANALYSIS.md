# Masterplan & Business Case — Analysis (2019/2020, Council-adopted)

**Source:** Ball Park Caravan Park Masterplan & Business Case (Nov 2019; adopted by
Council Oct 2020), per Schedule C data room. Layout/figures below transcribed from the
strategy review of that document — **verify against the source file before quoting in
any submission.**

> **Why this matters:** this is Council's *own adopted* position. Aligning to it scores
> directly on **Vision & Strategic Alignment (30%)**; the economic figures are the
> language Council decides on.

---

## Proposed future layout (consultants' concept)

**Northern Precinct** — 99 large tourist sites · 5 ensuite caravan sites + boat storage ·
new amenities hub · new camp kitchen · new entry precinct

**Central Precinct** — 32 large tourist sites · 20 relocated/upgraded cabins · 4 ensuite
caravan sites · new guest lounge over the river

**Southern Precinct** — 119 large tourist sites · 15 ensuite caravan sites · 5 premium
cabins over the lagoon/river · new amenities hub · playground · jumping pillow · pump
track · social spaces

**Indicative totals:** ~**250 tourist sites** · ~**25 cabins** (20 upgraded + 5 premium) ·
**24 ensuite caravan sites** → ~**300 accommodation sites/units**.

## Capacity reality check (vs current approval)

| | Current (S68 ATO) | Masterplan ambition | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total sites/units | **139** (123 camping incl. 97 powered + 20 short-term) | ~**300** | ~**+160 (roughly double)** |

- The ~300-site figure is an **ambition, not an entitlement.** New capacity (overflow +
  southern precinct) requires **lessee-led DA + S68 + Crown Land approvals** — risk and
  cost on us (EOI is explicit).
- **Flood Study (data room) governs buildable yield**, especially the riverfront/southern
  precinct and floor levels. Treat headline counts as flood-informed targets.

## The economic anchor (Council's currency)

- Business case projected local economic impact rising from **~$2.88M/yr → ~$5.6M/yr**
  (**≈ +$2.72M/yr**), via visitor nights, jobs and regional spend.
- **Use it as Council's own adopted figure**, not as an Aluxria projection. At Stage 1,
  reference it and the drivers qualitatively; reserve *our* quantified modelling for
  Stage 2. **Do not fabricate economic projections.**

## Positioning insight (the winning frame)

**Not** a luxury resort that competes with Council's expectations. **Instead:** a
**modern, high-capacity destination tourism park that delivers the adopted Masterplan
and its $5.6M economic target — faster and to a higher standard — via precision-built
modular accommodation, with a premium accommodation *layer* and staged experiential
upside.**

- Premium is the **yield enhancer**, not the headline. Calibrate submission tone so
  Aluxria's premium identity reads as *quality within a tourism park*, not "resort."
- The 2019 plan predates the growth in **remote/extended-stay, premium and experiential**
  travel — our enhancements *build on* the plan, not replace it (scores on **Concept
  Innovation 20%**).

## Commercial note — the revenue-profile shift

Moving from annual vans to tourism lifts yield but increases seasonality. The **operating
partner** and the **21-year viability model** must be comfortable with that profile.
Confirm the current mix from **FY2025 financials + RMS occupancy** (data room) — feeds
**go/no-go gate 1**.
