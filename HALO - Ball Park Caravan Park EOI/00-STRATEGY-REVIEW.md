# Strategy Review — Ball Park Caravan Park Redevelopment Strategy (v1)

**Reviewed:** 30 May 2026 · **Source strategy:** "Preliminary Opportunity Assessment &
Consortium Development Plan" (S. Phillips, Aluxria / Rock Solid Design & Construction)
**Benchmark:** official EOI **2026-305** (filed in `1. Council Documents`).

> Purpose: an honest, EOI-grounded critique to sharpen the strategy before the go/no-go
> decision. Strengths are real; the issues are fixable.

---

## Overall

A strong internal foundation. Vision, consortium logic and staging are sound, and the
"destination, not just a place to stay" positioning aligns closely with Council's
stated objectives. Three things need correcting before it becomes a credible basis for
a Stage 1 submission — one of them (the lease term) is material.

## What's strong — keep it

- **Vision ↔ objective alignment.** Matches Council's language (premier destination,
  tourism activation, diversified mix, sustainability). This is the natural high-scorer
  against *Vision & Strategic Alignment (30%)*.
- **Correct diagnosis that a consortium is required.** Capability & Experience is the
  heaviest criterion at **35%** and demands a proven operating/redevelopment track
  record with references. A consortium with a genuine operator is the structural answer.
- **Sound staging** that respects Council's "operate while developing the overflow"
  requirement.
- **Rock Solid Design & Construction** gives citable construction-delivery capability —
  real evidence for the "Redevelopment Partner" role.

## Critical issue — fix first: the 50–60 year lease

The strategy assumes a **50–60 year** stewardship lease. **That term is not available
in this process**, and proposing it risks signalling that the EOI hasn't been absorbed —
costly against a 35%-weighted capability criterion.

- EOI offers **21 years initial; 30 years *potential*, Ministerial approval *not
  guaranteed*.**
- Proponents are instructed (in capitals) to be **viable on the 21-year term**, treating
  30 as upside.
- **Crown land** (Reserve 80910) under the *Crown Land Management Act 2016* — 50–60 yrs
  is not in Council's gift here.

**Action:** retain the multi-generational *stewardship philosophy*, but express it
**within 21→30 years** via reinvestment commitments, a maintenance fund, and Aluxria
Care lifecycle stewardship. Model viability on **21 years**.

## Other calibrations (priority order)

1. **Timeline realism.** EOI closes **5:00pm Fri 26 June**. A binding consortium by then
   is unrealistic; at Stage 1 you need **named partners with LOIs / in-principle
   interest**, not signed deals. Decide now whether an operator (or credible LOI) is
   reachable before 26 June — it materially affects the 35% score.
2. **Drop Reflections Holidays from the partner list.** It is a **NSW Crown land manager
   (government entity)**, not a private consortium partner — more likely a competitor.
   Keep Hampshire, NRMA Parks & Resorts; add Discovery, Ingenia, Tasman, BIG4/G'Day.
3. **Floating accommodation = aspiration to investigate, not a committed Stage 3.** Best
   differentiator *and* highest regulatory risk (NSW Maritime, WaterNSW, Crown, MDBA,
   flood). EOI puts **all approval risk/cost on the lessee**. Include as upside; don't
   make the bid depend on it.
4. **Reframe due diligence from "investigate" to "retrieve & analyse."** Most DD items
   sit in the **Electronic Data Room** (Schedule C): Draft Lease, Flood Study,
   Financials, RMS Occupancy FY2025, Masterplan & Business Case, stamped plans.
5. **Add the Council-owned cabins decision.** C1–C20 valued **>$500k**, return expected —
   must state **buy or lease**. Missing from v1; it's a required element of the offer.
6. **Calibrate one phrase.** "Australia's premier… destination" edges into hype; use
   "a flagship Murray River destination" — ambitious but defensible.

## Make the go/no-go concrete — proceed only if all three are YES

1. **Viability** — does the model work on a **21-year** term (30 as upside), absorbing
   lessee-borne development cost on the unapproved overflow area?
2. **Capability** — can we field a **credible operator (or LOI)** by 26 June to satisfy
   the 35% criterion?
3. **Appetite** — is there an **investment partner** willing to fund a multi-$M,
   long-but-finite lease?

## Reordered immediate sequence

1. RSVP + attend the **3 June site inspection** (sole viewing).
2. Pull the **data room**; answer DD from primary sources.
3. **Operator outreach this week** (35% gate + binding timeline constraint).
4. Reframe the lease ask to **21→30**; run a viability sanity-check.
5. Then draft the **10-page Stage 1 concept**, mapped to 35/30/20/15.
