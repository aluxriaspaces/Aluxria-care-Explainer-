# 4. Tourism Market

**Purpose:** Size the demand, benchmark the competition, and read the community/
political environment — the evidence base for revenue assumptions and positioning.

## Capture
**Market (§5)**
- [ ] Corowa annual / overnight / caravan visitor numbers
- [ ] Occupancy rates — Corowa, Yarrawonga, Echuca, Mulwala, Albury
- [ ] Seasonal demand (summer, school holidays, events e.g. Corowa Swim-In, races)

**Competitors (§6)**
- [ ] Direct parks (Corowa, Mulwala, Yarrawonga): cabins, nightly rates, occupancy,
      reviews
- [ ] Premium / glamping / river accommodation in the region

**Community & political (§9)**
- [ ] What Council actually wants (from reports)
- [ ] Community sentiment (local news — support vs opposition)
- [ ] Economic objectives (tourism, jobs, activation)

## Inputs
economy.id (Federation), Destination NSW regional stats, Tourism Research Australia,
operator websites/OTAs for rates, local news (Border Mail, Daily Advertiser).

## Maps to checklist
§5, §6, §9.
