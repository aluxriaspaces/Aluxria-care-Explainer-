# Aluxria — Defence Capability Quad Chart

A single-page (A4 landscape) capability quad chart for Aluxria, targeted at
**Australian Defence** procurement. Standard four-quadrant layout:

1. **The capability** — what we deliver
2. **Defence value** — why it matters (rapid deployment, austere/northern, enduring asset, HADR)
3. **Technical approach** — specification & standards
4. **Delivery & sovereignty** — sovereign / First Nations–owned credentials & contact

The positioning leans on Aluxria's grounded strengths: sovereign, First
Nations–owned, Supply Nation registered, NCC-certified, BAL-FZ and cyclone-rated,
50-year relocatable assets — mapped to Defence needs (Indigenous Procurement
Policy, Australian Industry Capability, base regeneration, HADR).

## Regenerating the PDF

Produced from `index.html` via headless Chromium (A4 landscape, backgrounds on).
Any browser "print to PDF" with landscape orientation, background graphics on and
no margins reproduces it.

## To confirm before external release

Grounded in Aluxria's published positioning. The following are marked
*"to confirm"* on the chart and must be completed before issue:

- ABN and enquiry contact (name / email / phone)
- Defence security & quality credentials — DISP (Defence Industry Security
  Program) membership and ISO 9001 are shown as *to confirm*; do not present
  these as held until verified
- Supply Nation registration number

Cost and schedule are intentionally shown as "provided on request".

## Retargeting

This chart targets Australian Defence. It can be adapted to US DoD / SBIR
conventions (TRL line, period-of-performance, contract vehicle) on request.
