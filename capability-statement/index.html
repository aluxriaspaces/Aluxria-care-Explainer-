Precision-built, NCC-certified accommodation for Australia's renewable energy, resources, infrastructure and community-housing programs.

Aluxria designs, manufactures and supplies relocatable modular buildings engineered for a 50-year service life and Australian environmental extremes. 01
What we do

Design & manufacture

Modular accommodation designed and built to the National Construction Code. S70 and S40 systems, BAL-FZ bushfire and cyclone-rated, engineered for remote and regional Australian conditions. Digitally gated factory quality assurance prior to dispatch.

02
How we deliver

Supply, install & scale

Crane-liftable, pier and screw-pile foundations — no permanent slab, no rezoning. Staged quarterly delivery aligned to workforce ramp-up. Production capacity to 100 units per month, extendable through consortium partners for large programs.

03

Lifecycle stewardship

Every building carries an Aluxria Care Asset Passport and transparent Care Score, backed by a 7-to-10+ year warranty and a 50-year service architecture. Fully relocatable and re-deployable — engineered for reuse across multiple project lifecycles.

The organisation

Who Aluxria is

Aluxria is a First Nations–led modular construction business specialising in precision-built, relocatable accommodation for major Australian projects. We combine controlled factory manufacture with a lifecycle stewardship framework, and we deliver large programs through the First Nations–led HALO Village Delivery Consortium. A significant share of Aluxria's commercial profit funds the HALO Foundation's regional housing and First Nations community programs — commercial delivery and community benefit are structured as a single loop.

Consortium delivery

Modular partner within the HALO Village Delivery Consortium — a First Nations–owned principal contractor, engineering and prefabrication capacity, and specialist civil and renewable-energy infrastructure partners.

Sectors served

Renewable energy, mining and resources, government and social infrastructure, essential-worker and affordable housing, eco-tourism and First Nations community housing.

Geography

Remote and regional Australia. Active engagement across NSW (Hunter, Central West), the Northern Territory and the Northern Rivers, with capability nationwide.
